Two years ago I was going through what was serious episodes of stress. It manifested in night grinding or what they call bruxism. I'm way much better than I was two years ago. I'm blessed with a family that I love, great friends and I couldn't ask for more. Being the person I am it was too difficult even for my wife to help. I tell little and generally believe the change will always happen from within. I take it as an obligation on me to share with others just because I owe it to them to know what I'm going through. Not that I will expect an actual help in anyway or sort.  

My point of this post is to say: it is okay to be a pussy. There's no shame in that. But maybe let me implore you on my journey. Maybe it can help. Maybe reading something human can be better than your chatgpt.  I want to say first that the story or the specifics can be too boring. I don't think that will help much. But let me explain that. Whatever is making you stressed, whatever the things that causing you to be so that won't be the important part of this journey. Mine for example were revolving around family safety, job security, and some mundane day to day stuff. The boring part is that ALL of those are still there they didn't change. Yet I started the article was past present: "I was going through serious episodes of stress". What matters more in my opinion is that way I approached those causes. And that takes us to the ultimate point this post is trying to make: it is okay to be a pussy. I conceded that I'm not going to resolve any of those issues. That I will have to learn to deal with them. Some of them turns out to be easier to deal with. And for others I just learned to live with them. I still would get some sort of sleep paralysis (less often than before though) and I'd still have night terrors. But maybe the part that had enhanced _is_ how I deal with those. I got better at handling things so they won't lead into those episodes. Again there are no tricks there. It is like playing in a football match and you have only 10 players because one got a red card. You cannot undo that, you just _plan around it_. Yeah the odds are not in your favor, but not doing so (not conceding that you have a one fewer player and not planning for this reality) the outcome of it will be 100% not in your favor. 

So why did I say it is okay to be a chicken? Because what i described above kinda seems courageous to me. I think because I was initially holding it wrong. I thought that I will just fight my way through these episodes of stress and I would emerge victoreous. That was the courageous part I was after. Do you have night terror? Oh for fuck sake man up. NIHGT teeth grinding? ARE you f-ing kidding me. I was envisioning a figure of my uncle who sacrificed everything to support my mother and me. My grandpa as well. I wanted to be like them. I was always asking myself how do you dare not able to endure these shit? Do you even know how those two greats were enduring. It turns out I was hurting myself more than I was helping it. And those two were just better men than me.  That being a pussy was a better strategy in fact. Okay you have this and that problem. They are happening because of this and that. How can we divide and conquer? How can we take them one crisis at a time?
